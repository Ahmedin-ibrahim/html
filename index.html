About football

Football, also known as soccer in some countries, is played between two teams of eleven players each. The objective is to score goals by getting the ball into the opposing team's net. The game is played on a rectangular field, known as a pitch, with a goal at each end.

Football is very popular even reaching over 4 billion fans worldwide.

Ronaldo is the GOAT (Greatest of All Time). He has over 900 career goals. He has won 5 ballon d'or awards. He has won 4 Ucl with Real Madrid and 1 with Manchester United. He has won 1 Euro with Portugal. He is the all time top scorer for Portugal and Real Madrid. He is the only player to win the Champions League with 3 different clubs.
